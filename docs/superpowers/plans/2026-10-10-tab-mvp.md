# Tab MVP Implementation Plan

> Execute in this session with superpowers:executing-plans; test domain behavior first, then one fresh review.

**Goal:** Deliver a local installable receipt-parser Android MVP from the Linear tickets.
**Architecture:** Pure Kotlin domain; Room repository with reactive ledger; Compose screens and Hilt view models; ML Kit capture pipeline.
**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, Compose, Room, Hilt, ML Kit, DataStore, WorkManager.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-tab-mvp-design.md

## Global constraints
- minSdk 26, integer centavos, PHP, English, no backend, no remote repository.
- Robert is not a friend; rounding and reconciliation residual go to Robert.
- Preserve payment history on receipt edits/deletion. Draft recovery and atomic saves.
- Real-receipt and physical-device acceptance remain explicitly unverified.

## Review focus
- Invalid/huge monetary entry must not silently become zero or overflow.
- Fee-excluded people, zero item weights and negative residual must be explained correctly.
- Editing attendee lists must not resurrect removed assignments or lose mine-only semantics.
- Cancellation, rotation and process death must preserve or explicitly discard work.
- Old paid receipts must not cause false overdue reminders; future payments rejected.
